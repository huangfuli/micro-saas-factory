# SupplyExtract Validation Plan

## Test 1 — Workflow value
Invite operations/admin users who currently copy supplier PDF data into spreadsheets to process one real non-sensitive supplier document.

Pass signal: 10 qualified users complete extraction and at least 3 request continued access or pricing.

## Test 2 — Willingness to pay
Show the real Lemon Squeezy subscription price only after the product and price are configured.

Pass signal: at least 3 paid starts or explicit purchase commitments from 15 qualified conversations.

## Non-goals
Do not use fake testimonials, fake company logos, fabricated waitlist counts, fabricated savings percentages or invented accuracy metrics.
